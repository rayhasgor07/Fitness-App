Glute Bridge

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor. Lift your hips to form a straight line from shoulders to knees, then lower.

Mountain Climbers

In a plank position, drive one knee toward your chest, alternating legs quickly to activate the hamstrings.

Lateral Leg Swings

Stand on one leg and swing the other back and forth to engage the hamstrings and hip flexors.

Standing Leg Curls

Stand and bend one knee to bring your heel toward your glutes, then lower and repeat with the other leg.
