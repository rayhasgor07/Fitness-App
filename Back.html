Superman Holds

Lie on your stomach, lift your arms, chest, and legs off the ground, hold, then lower slowly.

Wall Angels

Stand with your back against a wall and slowly raise and lower your arms like a shoulder press.

Bridge Hold

Lie on your back, plant your feet, and lift your hips up, squeezing your glutes and lower back.

Good Mornings

Stand with hands behind your head, hinge at the hips until your torso is parallel to the ground, then return to standing.
