Glute Bridge

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor. Lift your hips to form a straight line from shoulders to knees, then lower.

Donkey Kicks

On all fours, kick one leg back and upward while keeping the knee bent at 90 degrees, then lower and repeat.

Fire Hydrants

On all fours, lift one leg out to the side while keeping the knee bent, then lower and repeat.

Squats

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, lower your body as if sitting back in a chair, then stand back up.

Lunges

Step forward with one leg, lower your body into a 90-degree angle at both knees, then push back up and alternate legs.

Wall Sit

Sit against a wall with your knees at a 90-degree angle and hold to engage the glutes.

Clamshell

Lie on your side with knees bent. Open and close your knees while keeping your feet together to activate the glutes and hip abductors.
