Push-Ups

Keep your body straight, lower until your chest nearly touches the floor, then push back up.

Incline Push-Ups

Place your hands on an elevated surface to target the lower chest.

Decline Push-Ups

Elevate your feet to emphasize the upper chest.

Wide Push-Ups

Place your hands wider than shoulder-width to engage the outer chest.

Diamond Push-Ups

Bring your hands together in a diamond shape to target the inner chest and triceps.

Archer Push-Ups

Extend one arm out while lowering toward the other arm for increased chest activation.

Clapping Push-Ups

Explosively push off the ground and clap before landing back in push-up position.
