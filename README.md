# ⚡ Fitness App

A full-featured fitness web app built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Firebase. Designed for real users — track workouts, calories, nutrition, body metrics, and get AI-generated workout plans, all in one clean mobile-friendly interface.

🔗 **Live App:** [fitness-app-3a698.web.app](https://fitness-app-3a698.web.app)

---

## Features

### 🎯 Goal-Based System
Choose your fitness goal on first launch — Cut, Bulk, Maintain, Grow Glutes, or Get Shredded. The entire app adapts: recommended plans, daily tips, macro splits, and workout suggestions all change based on your goal.

### 🤖 AI Workout Generator
Generate personalized workout plans based on your goal, fitness level, and available equipment.

### 🔥 Calorie Tracker
Log your daily meals and track calorie intake. Supports custom entries and real-time totals.

### 🧮 Fitness Calculators (7 tools)
- **BMI Calculator** — with health status classification
- **Calorie Calculator** — TDEE with activity level, cut/bulk targets
- **Water Intake** — daily hydration based on weight and activity
- **Macro Split** — protein, carbs, and fat breakdown per goal
- **Ideal Body Weight** — with healthy range
- **Body Fat % (Navy Method)** — with category classification
- **One Rep Max (1RM)** — with 70%, 80%, and 90% training zones

All calculators support **metric and imperial units** with a global toggle.

### 🍗 Nutrition Guide
Goal-specific nutrition advice, meal planning guidance, and hydration tips.

### 📊 Progress Tracker
Log your weight over time, track streak and active days, and monitor your body changes.

### 🧘 Yoga & Flexibility
A dedicated yoga section with guided flows for recovery and mobility.

### 📏 Height Tips
Evidence-based guide on maximizing height potential — sleep, diet, stretching, and activity. Gender-specific images and advice.

### 💪 Muscle Guide
Targeted workout guides for specific muscle groups including chest, hamstrings, glutes, and more.

### 👤 Profile & Auth
- Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication
- Gender selection that personalizes images and calculations throughout the app
- Persistent data saved locally per user

### 📱 Mobile First
Built entirely mobile-first with a bottom navigation bar, smooth scrolling, and a clean dark UI with neon accents.
