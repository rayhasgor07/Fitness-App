Crunches

Lie on your back, knees bent, curl your upper body toward your knees, then lower slowly.

Leg Raises

Lie on your back, legs straight, lift them to a 90-degree angle, then slowly lower without touching the floor.

Russian Twists

Sit on the floor, lean back slightly, rotate your torso side to side, tapping the floor each time.

Plank

Hold a forearm or full plank position, keeping your body straight from head to heels.

Mountain Climbers

In a plank position, drive one knee toward your chest, alternating quickly.
