STRETCHING

Why Stretching Matters

Stretching increases flexibility, boosts blood flow, reduces injury risk, improves recovery, decreases soreness, and helps your joints move through a full range of motion. It also promotes relaxation and reduces muscle tension.

Cobra Pose

Lie on your stomach, place hands under shoulders, and lift your chest while keeping elbows slightly bent.

Child’s Pose

Sit on your heels, extend arms forward, and lower your chest to the mat with your forehead resting down.

Crescent Lunge

Step one foot forward, bend the front knee at 90°, and raise arms overhead while keeping the back leg straight.

Pigeon Pose

Bring one knee forward, extend the other leg back, and lower your torso over the bent leg for a deep hip stretch.

Bridge Pose

Lie on your back, bend knees, place feet hip-width apart, and lift your hips while pressing into your heels.

Spinal Twist

Lie on your back, bend one knee across your body, and gently twist while keeping shoulders grounded.
