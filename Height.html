GENETICS & GROWTH

Height is mostly genetics (around 80%), but the remaining 20% is influenced by your lifestyle, habits, and environment.

SLEEP

Sleep is the most important factor for growth. Aim for 9–10 hours every night. Deep sleep (10 PM–2 AM) is when your body releases the most growth hormone. Avoid screens 1 hour before bed and keep your room dark and cool.

STAY ACTIVE

Sprinting, swimming, basketball, and explosive exercises boost HGH and strengthen bones. Many athletes grow taller because of their training intensity and posture improvements.

STRETCHING

Stretching improves posture and decompresses the spine. Bar hangs, cobra stretch, yoga, and gravity-based stretches can help you stand taller and support growth during puberty.

DIET

A proper diet fuels growth. Prioritize protein, calcium, vitamin D, magnesium, zinc, and potassium. Foods like bananas, milk, eggs, chicken, fish, nuts, leafy greens, and whole grains support bone strength and HGH production. Avoid caffeine and processed sugar as they interfere with growth and recovery.
