Jump Squats

Perform a squat and explode upward into a jump, landing softly back into a squat position.

Lunges

Step forward into a lunge, lower your hips until your front thigh is parallel to the ground, then push back up and alternate legs.

Wall Sit

Sit against a wall with your knees at 90 degrees and hold to engage your quadriceps.

High Knees

Jog in place, lifting your knees high to activate the quadriceps with each step.
